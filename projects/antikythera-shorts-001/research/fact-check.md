# Fact-Check — The 2,000-Year-Old Computer (Shorts 001)

Status: **research v1, 2026-11-25.** Every claim the video makes (spoken, on-screen, in the description) must map to a row here.

## How to read this file

**Confidence classes**

| Class | Meaning | Allowed phrasing |
|---|---|---|
| **Established** | Directly supported by surviving physical evidence or by several independent primary/peer-reviewed sources | Plain statement |
| **Strongly supported** | Peer-reviewed consensus or the best reading of the evidence, but not directly observable | "probably", "researchers found", "evidence suggests" |
| **Reconstructed / inferred** | A published model or inference filling a gap in the evidence | "a 2021 reconstruction proposes…", "reconstructed", "artist's reconstruction" |
| **Uncertain / disputed** | Experts disagree or evidence is missing | Say nothing, or say it is debated |

**What we actually read** (this matters; see `sources.md` for detail)

| Tag | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `[GOV]` | Greek Ministry of Culture page, read in full |
| `[SR21]` | Scientific Reports 2021 (Freeth et al.), **full text read** (open access) |
| `[NA18-abs]` | Nature Astronomy 2018, **abstract, figure captions and reference list only** (paywalled) |
| `[N06-abs]` | Nature 2006, **abstract and references only** (paywalled) |
| `[UCL21]` | **NOT READ.** The UCL page returned HTTP 403 on every attempt. Nothing below relies on it. |
| `[2nd]` | Secondary summary (an encyclopaedia page read via a sub-agent). A lead only; never sufficient for a claim. |

Items marked **VERIFY** must be checked against a full text before they appear in a final graphic.

## The evidence boundary (use this for every visual)

| Tier | What it is | Examples | On-screen treatment |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A: surviving evidence** | Physical remains and their inscriptions | 82 fragments, 30 gears, Fragment A's layered gearing, Saros and Metonic dials, lunar pin-and-slot | Solid, aged bronze; real damage allowed |
| **B: inferred from evidence** | Required by the surviving parts, with strong agreement | The crank drive, the case, missing portions of the back dials | Clean matte bronze, no patina. Described as "reconstruction" |
| **C: published hypothesis** | A proposal that fits the data but cannot be confirmed | The 2021 front planetary layers (34 gears), the Dragon Hand | Ghosted, translucent Scientific Cyan. Legend: **RECONSTRUCTION** |

The legend **SURVIVING EVIDENCE / RECONSTRUCTION** appears in scene 06 and in any shot that mixes tiers.

## Claims the video must never make

- That it was a **modern or digital computer**, or that it "ran software".
- That any gear arrangement shown is "how it was built", beyond Tier A.
- That it was **the first computer**.
- A single **construction year**, or a **month** of recovery.
- A **maker, workshop or place of origin**.
- That visuals are **archival footage** of the wreck, the divers, or the machine in action.
- **Unsourced numbers** such as gear tooth counts, diameters or accuracy percentages (see open items).
